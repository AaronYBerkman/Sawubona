The SASL alphabet, by hand

Twenty-four static letters, each drawn from one real held frame of a Real SASL presenter. Every hand is a right hand, seen as you would see it facing the signer. J and Z move, so they are not drawn here.

SASL T is the index finger folded over the thumb tip, not the ASL T. Source frames and presenters are listed in hands.json. Drawings made by tools/make-hands.py from MediaPipe landmarks of the held frames.
